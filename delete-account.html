Request Account Deletion

GoGet respects your right to control your personal information. If you no longer wish to use GoGet, you may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your GoGet account using the following steps:

  1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Your email application will open with a pre-filled account deletion request.
  3. Send the request using the email address associated with your GoGet account where possible.
  4. Include your registered phone number or email address so that we can identify your account.
  5. GoGet may contact you to verify that you are the owner of the account before processing the deletion request.
Request Account Deletion

If the button does not work on your device, you can send an email directly to support@goget.co.tz with the subject "GoGet Account Deletion Request".

Information You Should Provide

To help us identify your account, please provide:

  • Your full name
  • Your registered email address, if applicable
  • Your registered phone number, if applicable

Do not send your password, PIN, OTP, payment card number, mobile money PIN, or other sensitive authentication information.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After your identity and account ownership have been verified, GoGet will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account where applicable.

This may include:

  • Account profile information
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Saved addresses
  • Stored location-related account information
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Information We May Retain

Some information may need to be retained after account deletion where reasonably necessary or required by applicable law.

This may include certain:

  • Transaction records
  • Payment records
  • Financial and accounting records
  • Fraud prevention records
  • Security records
  • Records relating to disputes or legal obligations

Information retained for these purposes will not be used for unrelated purposes and will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary or legally required.

What Happens After Deletion?

Once your account has been deleted, you may lose access to information and services associated with the account.

Account deletion may be permanent, and deleted information may not be recoverable.

Important: Please make sure you no longer require access to your GoGet account, active orders, receipts, or other account information before requesting permanent account deletion.

Processing Your Request

We will review account deletion requests and may contact you if additional information is reasonably necessary to verify account ownership or process the request.

We aim to process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud prevention, and regulatory requirements.
